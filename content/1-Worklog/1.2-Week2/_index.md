### Week 2 Objectives:

* Learn about AWS Budgets and how to manage and control costs associated with AWS services.
* Practice creating and configuring various types of budgets (Cost Budget, Usage Budget, RI Budget, and Savings Plans Budget).
* Set up alert thresholds to monitor costs and AWS resource usage levels.

### Week 2 Achievements:

* Understood the role and functionality of AWS Budgets in managing and controlling AWS costs.
* Gained hands-on experience in creating and configuring Cost Budgets and Usage Budgets, including setting budget alert thresholds.

* Mastered the procedure for reviewing and cleaning up budgets after finishing the practical exercises.
