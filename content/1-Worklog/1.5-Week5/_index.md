### Week 5 Objectives:

* Learn about Amazon EC2 Auto Scaling Groups and how to deploy auto-scaling applications on AWS.
* Gain hands-on experience in building network infrastructure, deploying the FCAJ Management application, and configuring Elastic Load Balancing.
* Explore and practice various system scaling strategies, including Manual Scaling, Scheduled Scaling, Dynamic Scaling, and Predictive Scaling.

### Outcomes for Week 5:

* Understood the role of Amazon EC2 Auto Scaling Groups and the benefits of automatically scaling AWS infrastructure.
* Gained hands-on experience in building a VPC network, deploying applications on EC2, and connecting to an Amazon RDS database.
* Learned how to create AMIs, Launch Templates, and Target Groups, and how to configure an Application Load Balancer.
* Understood and practiced Manual Scaling, Scheduled Scaling, Dynamic Scaling, and Predictive Scaling strategies.
