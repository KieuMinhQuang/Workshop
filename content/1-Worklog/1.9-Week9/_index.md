### Week 9 Objectives:

* Explore AWS cost-optimization models, including Savings Plans, Reserved Instances, and Reserved DB Instances.
* Distinguish between the various types of Savings Plans and Reserved Instances, and understand the scope of application for each.
* Learn how to utilize Savings Plans Recommendations to analyze usage patterns and select the most appropriate cost-saving options.
* Understand the process for purchasing and managing commitment-based plans for EC2 and Amazon RDS resources.
